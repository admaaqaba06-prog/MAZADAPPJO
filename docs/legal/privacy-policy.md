## 2. What data we collect

**You provide directly:**
- **Phone number** — your primary identity on the Platform (verified by one-time
  SMS code).
- **Name and city** — required to complete your profile and to attribute bids
  and orders.
- **Email address** — optional; used as a contact/receipt address if you provide
  it.
- **Google account basic profile** — if you sign in with Google.
- **Payment proof** — screenshots/images of your CliQ transfers that you upload
  to confirm payment.
- **Seller information** — for sellers: item details, media (photos/video), and
  ownership/legality attestation; identity/verification data where required.
- **Delivery details** — the address and contact you provide for an order.

**Collected automatically:**
- **Device and session information** — browser, platform, app version, and
  activity timestamps (e.g. last-seen), used for security and support.
- **Approximate technical data** — such as IP address, for security and
  fraud-prevention.
- **Activity on the Platform** — bids, wins, orders, and interactions.

## 3. How we use your data

- To operate the Platform: register you, verify your identity, let you bid, and
  process auctions, orders, and the payment-hold/release described in our Terms.
- To communicate with you: order status, payment reminders, disputes, and
  service notifications (including via WhatsApp and/or SMS where you have
  provided your number).
- For safety and fraud prevention: enforcing bidding rules, detecting abuse,
  and blocking accounts that breach our Terms (e.g. non-payment).
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.

## 5. Data security

## 6. Sharing your data

We share your data only:
- With **sellers/buyers** to the extent needed to complete a transaction (e.g.

  confirmed**, so the item can be delivered).
- With **our service providers** (§4), under agreements limiting their use of it.
- With **authorities** where required by law, or to prevent fraud or harm.
