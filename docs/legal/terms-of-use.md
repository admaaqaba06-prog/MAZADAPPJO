## 3. What Mazad is — and is not

Mazad is a **marketplace and intermediary** that hosts time-limited auctions of
items listed by sellers. Each listing is submitted by a seller (self-serve or
via Mazad's concierge intake) and **reviewed and approved by the Mazad team
before it goes live**. Listings include photos and/or video and item details.

Mazad is **not** the seller of items listed by third-party sellers, except where
expressly stated. Mazad reviews listings for basic standards but does not
independently guarantee the authenticity, quality, or description of every item;
buyer protection is provided through the payment-hold and dispute process
described in Section 7.

## 4. Membership & bidding

- Bidding requires an **active paid membership**. Membership tiers are:
  **1 JOD / month**, **4 JOD / 6 months**, and **7 JOD / year** (or as updated
  on the Platform).
- Membership fees are paid via **CliQ** to Mazad and are **non-refundable** once
  the membership period begins, except as required by applicable law.
- Membership grants the ability to bid; it does not guarantee winning any
  auction.

### Bidding rules

- Each bid is a **binding offer to purchase** at the bid amount. By placing a
  bid you commit to pay if you win.
- Bids must meet the minimum increment shown. The first bid may equal the asking
  price; subsequent bids must exceed the current price by at least the minimum
  increment.
- Auctions are time-limited. A bid placed in the final seconds may extend the
  auction end time (anti-sniping). The winner is the highest valid bid when the
  auction closes, as determined by Mazad's servers, which are authoritative.

## 5. Fees

- **Buyer's premium:** 5% of the winning bid, added to the amount the winning
  buyer pays.
- **Seller's commission:** 5% of the winning bid, deducted from the seller's
  proceeds. The seller receives the remaining 95%.
- Membership fees are separate from and in addition to the above.

All fees are stated in Jordanian Dinars (JOD).

## 6. Payment

- The winning buyer must pay the total due (winning bid + 5% buyer's premium)
  **via CliQ to Mazad within 24 hours** of the auction closing.
- **Payment is made to Mazad, not directly to the seller.**
- If a winning buyer fails to pay within the required window, Mazad may cancel
  the order, **suspend or block the buyer's account**, and/or restrict future
  bidding.

## 7. How buyer protection works (payment hold)

This is the core of how Mazad protects buyers, and it defines what protection is
and is not offered:

- When you win and pay, **Mazad holds your payment.** Your payment is **not**
  released to the seller at the time you pay.
- Mazad releases the funds to the seller **only after you (the buyer) receive
  the item, verify it, and approve the release.**
- If there is a problem, discrepancy, or dispute **before you approve release**,
  you may open a dispute and Mazad will mediate. If the dispute is resolved in
  your favour before release, your held funds are returned to you.
- **There are no refunds after a sale is completed** (i.e., after you have
  received the item and approved release of funds to the seller). Once you
  confirm receipt and release the funds, the sale is final.
